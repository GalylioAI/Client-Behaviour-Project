# Smart Actions

Smart Actions is the automation and intelligence area of BehaviourAI. It follows the flow: Score, Recommend, Act, Measure.

Score means identifying visitors and sessions with high purchase intent.

Recommend means selecting relevant products based on the customer's journey.

Act means preparing outreach, such as email drafts for abandoned carts or repeated product interest.

Measure means tracking whether these actions lead to return visits, clicks, carts, or purchases.

Current implemented Smart Actions:
- Purchase Intent: scores sessions and visitors using rules-based behaviour signals.
- Recommendations: generates product recommendation candidates and prepared email drafts.

Current safety mode:
- Sending is draft-only.
- No email or WhatsApp message should be sent automatically until consent rules, unsubscribe handling, cooldowns, and provider configuration are implemented.

Future Smart Actions may include WhatsApp messages, price drop alerts, back-in-stock alerts, win-back campaigns, and GenAI-written business recommendations.

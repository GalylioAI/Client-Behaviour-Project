# BehaviourAI Platform Overview

BehaviourAI is a multi-tenant ecommerce behaviour intelligence platform. It collects website behaviour events from WordPress/WooCommerce and PrestaShop tracker plugins, processes them through a distributed data pipeline, stores analytics in ClickHouse, and presents business insights in the SaaS dashboard.

The platform helps business owners understand how visitors discover products, move through the funnel, abandon carts, complete purchases, and respond to product recommendations.

Core platform areas:
- Dashboard: business performance, revenue, conversion, trends, and action summaries.
- Live Events: accepted tracker events, freshness, event mix, and recent activity.
- Funnels: session movement from discovery to cart, checkout, and purchase.
- Audience: traffic, devices, loyalty, and customer signals.
- Products: product views, clicks, add-to-cart actions, and engagement.
- Smart Actions: purchase intent scoring, product recommendations, and prepared outreach.
- Reports: exports, trends, and reporting views.
- Sites: connected websites, tenant boundaries, and installation state.
- API Keys: public write keys and private server secrets.
- Pipelines: insights refresh jobs, data processing, and infrastructure health.
- Settings: site configuration and security settings.

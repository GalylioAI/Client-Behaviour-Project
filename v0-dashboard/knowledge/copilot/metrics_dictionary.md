# Metrics Dictionary

Sessions are visits or browsing journeys identified from tracker session IDs.

Visitors are distinct people or browser identities identified through visitor_id, customer_id, or related tracking identifiers.

Conversion rate is purchase sessions divided by total sessions in the selected analysis window.

Cart abandonment reflects users who added products to cart but did not complete a purchase in the observed journey.

Average order value is revenue divided by purchase sessions.

Revenue is based on captured purchase_completed events, adjusted where lifecycle reversal events are available.

Purchase intent score is a deterministic score from 0 to 100 based on product views, cart actions, checkout behaviour, session depth, recency, and whether the visitor already purchased.

Recommendation candidates are products selected from a visitor journey because the visitor viewed them repeatedly, clicked them, or added them to cart without a matching purchase.

Email drafts are prepared outreach messages. They are not sent automatically in the current version.

Referrers are browser referrers from document.referrer. Campaign sources are URL attribution parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign.
